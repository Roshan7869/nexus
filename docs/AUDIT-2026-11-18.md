## 7. 🔧 Fixed during this audit

**`tool_attention_v4.py` imported `sentence_transformers` at module scope.** On a
torch-free install that raised `ModuleNotFoundError` and the ISO lane logged
`ISO scoring skipped: sentence_transformers not installed` on **every** v4 route — v4
silently degraded to keyword-only matching. Fixed with a lazy fallback to the ONNX
encoder in `IsoEmbedder` (same 384-dim MiniLM, shared model cache). Verified:
`MODEL=_OnnxEncoder`, `compute_iso_scores(...)` → 127 scored resources.
